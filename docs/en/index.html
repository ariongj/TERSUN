Signature product

Light, shade and shelter – in one roof.

The louvres of the Bioclimatic System rotate at the touch of a button. Open them for sky and air, tilt them to block the midday sun, close them when it rains. The roof adapts to the weather – not the other way round.

  • Rotating louvres
  • Watertight when closed
  • Integrated LED
  • Motorised control
  • Single, double or modular
